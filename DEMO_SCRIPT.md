# ABHAYA SETU (अभय सेतु) — 3-Minute Hackathon Demonstration Script
**Tagline:** *Bridging Distress to Rescue*  
**Audience:** Hackathon Judges, Civic Product Architects, and Public Safety Observers  
**Target Duration:** ~180 seconds (3 minutes)

---

### Phase 1: Inception & Problem Statement (0:00 – 0:30)
- **Presenter Action:** Open the web browser at [http://localhost:5173/](http://localhost:5173/) on the Command Center tab.
- **Presenter Dialogue:**
  > *"Good morning judges. In high-density urban transit nodes—such as railway foot-overbridges or bus terminals—seconds make the difference between safety and tragedy. Yet traditional CCTV surveillance either leaves footage unmonitored or violates citizen privacy through mass cloud facial tracking.*
  > *Meet **ABHAYA SETU (अभय सेतु)**: an intelligent, privacy-first civic safety network designed to bridge distress to rescue. Notice the live operational telemetry: monitored safety poles across Guntur, Andhra Pradesh, ready patrol fleets, and our prominent DEMO MODE indicator."*

---

### Phase 2: Live Geographic Grid & Monitored Safety Nodes (0:30 – 0:50)
- **Presenter Action:** Point to the dark-mode OpenStreetMap canvas and click on **NODE-GNT-01** (Guntur RTC Bus Stand) or **NODE-GNT-02** (Railway FOB).
- **Presenter Dialogue:**
  > *"Here on our Guntur Operations Matrix, each autonomous safety pole operates localized acoustic sensor arrays and low-light optical keypoint tracking. Clicking any node reveals live telemetry: environmental decibel levels, sensor health, and localized actuator relays like our 2400-lumen emergency strobes and audio guidance beacons."*

---

### Phase 3: Triggering Multilingual Distress in Detection Lab (0:50 – 1:25)
- **Presenter Action:** Navigate to the **Detection Lab** in the sidebar. Select **Scenario A: Multilingual Distress Call at RTC Bus Stand**. Click **"Start Edge Analysis"**.
- **Presenter Dialogue:**
  > *"Let's simulate a commuter cornered at RTC Bus Stand Platform 4 yelling for help in Telugu: 'కాపాడండి!'.*
  > *Watch the real-time audio waveform and scanning reticle: the local acoustic processor captures the 89.4 dB harmonic spike and matches the Telugu distress keyword at 94% confidence. Concurrently, optical skeletal tracking registers defensive cornering. The deterministic edge fusion engine flags a High Severity event and routes it to our human operator desk."*

---

### Phase 4: Human-in-the-Loop Operator Verification & Patrol Dispatch (1:25 – 2:05)
- **Presenter Action:** Click **"View Created Incident"** (or switch to **Incident Manager**). Open the incident situation room. Click **"Verify Threat"**. Then select the nearest recommended unit (*Eagle-1* or *Cheetah-2*) and click **"Dispatch Unit"**.
- **Presenter Dialogue:**
  > *"Crucially, ABHAYA SETU enforces strict human-in-the-loop verification. No sirens or police dispatches occur automatically without operator review. Here is our automated Situation Report (SitRep) and acoustic/optical evidence clips.*
  > *We click 'Verify Threat', and our dispatch engine instantly calculates the nearest available patrol using Haversine routing. We dispatch Eagle-1 with a simulated ETA of ~2 minutes."*

---

### Phase 5: Tracking Response & De-escalation (2:05 – 2:30)
- **Presenter Action:** In Incident Management or Patrol Dispatch, click **"Mark Responding"**, then click **"Resolve Incident"**. Note the celebration confetti and state update.
- **Presenter Dialogue:**
  > *"The unit acknowledges dispatch and moves to 'Responding'. Once officers secure the scene, we mark it 'Resolved'. Notice that our system automatically deactivates the local strobe and returns the patrol vehicle back to the Available fleet pool—preventing duplicate assignments or orphan alerts."*

---

### Phase 6: False-Positive Suppression (The Festival Test) (2:30 – 2:50)
- **Presenter Action:** Return to **Detection Lab** and select **Scenario D: Harmless Loud Festival Celebration**. Click **"Start Edge Analysis"**.
- **Presenter Dialogue:**
  > *"Now for the real test: a Ganesh procession with loud dhol drums and firecrackers reaching 96 dB. Watch how our edge processor handles this: because the spectral analysis detects periodic 128 BPM festive rhythms and zero distress keywords, the classifier marks it as Benign Festive Noise. Zero panic alerts. Zero wasted emergency dispatches. Complete suppression of false positives."*

---

### Phase 7: Privacy-by-Design & Conclusion (2:50 – 3:00)
- **Presenter Action:** Click on the **Privacy Center** tab.
- **Presenter Dialogue:**
  > *"Finally, privacy is built into the architecture: zero continuous cloud video broadcasting, a 5-minute rolling RAM circular buffer that constantly overwrites unflagged data, and zero facial recognition or biometric profiling. ABHAYA SETU bridges distress to rescue—efficiently, autonomously, and ethically. Thank you!"*
